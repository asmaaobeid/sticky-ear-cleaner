Questions buyers ask

How does Sticky Ear Cleaner work?

The soft adhesive tip gently rotates in the outer ear canal so wax sticks to the tip and lifts out, instead of being pushed deeper like with many cotton swabs.

Is it painful?

It is meant for a gentle outer-ear clean with light rotation only. If you feel pain, stop immediately and consult a professional.

What if I have a lot of wax?

Use a fresh tip and repeat gently. Do not force or dig deep. For heavy buildup or hearing issues, see a doctor or clinic.

Single-use or reusable?

Single-use is best for hygiene. You can wash, blow-dry, and reuse a few times if the tip still sticks — never share used tips between people.
