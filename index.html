Questions & Lebanon delivery

How does Sticky Ear Cleaner work?

The soft adhesive tip gently rotates in the outer ear canal so wax sticks to the tip and lifts out, instead of being pushed deeper like with many cotton swabs.

Are my ears supposed to be dry?

Yes. Please make sure ears are dry before using for the best grip and a more comfortable experience.

Is it suitable for sensitive ears?

The tip is soft plastic / silica-gel style with a full wrap, designed for comfort. If you have an ear infection, perforated eardrum, or pain, stop and ask a healthcare professional before use.

Can it be reused?

You can wash off dirt with water and blow dry to help retain stickiness. For hygiene, single-use is ideal — especially between different people.
